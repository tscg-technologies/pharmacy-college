Academic Prerequisites

Eligibility Criteria for Indian Pharmacy Aspirants

General educational eligibility guidelines applicable to pharmaceutical courses across Indian educational systems:

For D.Pharm & B.Pharm

Relevant 10+2 science background with Physics and Chemistry as compulsory subjects along with Mathematics or Biology as applicable under relevant course regulations.

For M.Pharm (Postgraduate)

Relevant qualifying B.Pharm degree from a recognized institution with applicable entrance examination qualification or state counseling selection where required.

For Pharm.D (Doctor of Pharmacy)

Relevant qualifying 10+2 science background (PCM/PCB) or recognized D.Pharm qualification or other eligibility pathway as prescribed by applicable regulations.

Notice: Exact eligibility percentages, age criteria, approved intake, and entrance exam requirements vary by state examining authority and must be verified against current official gazette regulations.
