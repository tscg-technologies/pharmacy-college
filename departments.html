Department 01

Department of Pharmaceutics

Pharmaceutics is the science of dosage form design, transforming active drug molecules into stable, effective, and safe medications suitable for patient administration.

Tablet & Capsule Technology
Sustained Release Systems
Biopharmaceutics & PK
Sterile Injectable Formulations

Associated Facility: Pharmaceutics Laboratory & Machine Room.

Medicinal Compound Synthesis
Structure-Activity Studies
Organic Drug Chemistry
Drug Design Fundamentals

Associated Facility: Pharmaceutical Chemistry Laboratory.

Department of Pharmacology

Investigates the physiological and biochemical interactions between medications and living biological systems, assessing efficacy, mechanism, and toxicological thresholds.

Pharmacodynamics & PK
Experimental Bioassays
Toxicological Screening
Computer-Assisted Simulation

Associated Facility: Pharmacology Laboratory & Animal Simulation Lab.

Department of Pharmacognosy

Examines the identification, macroscopic/microscopic evaluation, extraction, and standardization of medicinal compounds sourced from natural plants, marine fauna, and minerals.

Medicinal Plant Taxonomy
Phytochemical Extraction
Herbal Drug Standardization
Natural Product Evaluation

Associated Facility: Pharmacognosy Laboratory & Herbal Garden.

UV-Vis Spectrophotometry
HPLC Chromatography
Assay Method Validation
Purity & Impurity Profiling
