Diploma Pathway

Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)

Duration: 2 Academic Years + Required Practical Training

An introductory professional pharmacy education pathway focused on foundational pharmaceutical knowledge and practical pharmacy training. The curriculum prepares students for drug dispensing, community healthcare assistance, and retail pharmaceutical administration.

Core Subjects Covered:

Pharmaceutics
Pharmaceutical Chemistry
Pharmacognosy
Human Anatomy & Physiology
Social Pharmacy
Pharmacy Practice

Eligibility Note: 10+2 science passing with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) or Biology (PCB) from an approved examination board.

Undergraduate Degree

Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)

Duration: 4 Academic Years (8 Semesters)

An undergraduate program focused on pharmaceutical sciences, drug development, formulation, quality, and pharmacy practice. The degree develops broad expertise encompassing medicinal chemical synthesis, bioassays, pharmacology, industrial manufacturing, and quality management.

Core Subjects Covered:

Pharmaceutics
Pharmaceutical Analysis
Medicinal Chemistry
Pharmacology
Pharmacognosy
Pharmaceutical Biotechnology
Industrial Pharmacy
Pharmacy Practice

Eligibility Note: 10+2 science passing with Physics and Chemistry as compulsory subjects along with Mathematics or Biology, meeting statutory criteria.

Postgraduate Degree

Master of Pharmacy (M.Pharm)

Duration: 2 Academic Years (4 Semesters)

A postgraduate pathway for advanced pharmaceutical learning and specialization. Students undertake rigorous course electives and a dedicated laboratory research dissertation under the mentorship of faculty guides.

Available Demonstration Specializations:

Pharmaceutics
Pharmacology
Pharmaceutical Chemistry
Pharmacognosy
Pharmaceutical Analysis
Pharmacy Practice

Eligibility Note: Recognized B.Pharm degree along with applicable entrance qualification per affiliating authority requirements.

Doctoral Professional Pathway

Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D)

Duration: 6 Years (5 Years Academic + 1 Year Hospital Residency)

A professional pharmacy education pathway with emphasis on clinical pharmacy, pharmacotherapy, medication management, and patient-oriented pharmacy education. Students gain intensive hospital clinical exposure in patient chart reviews, therapeutic drug monitoring, and clinical drug safety.

Core Learning Areas:

Clinical Pharmacy
Pharmacotherapeutics
Hospital Pharmacy
Clinical Research
Pharmacology
Pharmacy Practice

Eligibility Note: 10+2 science passing with PCM or PCB, or D.Pharm from an approved institution per prevailing regulations.
