Pharmaceutical Research Laboratory

Dedicated environment supporting postgraduate thesis dissertations, nanotechnology formulation exploration, and faculty-guided investigative drug studies.

Key Instrumentation: High-Performance Liquid Chromatograph (HPLC), Sonicator, Cold Centrifuge, Stability Chamber.

Laboratory Safety Standards & Good Practice Guidelines

Student learning occurs within structured safety frameworks prioritizing environmental responsibility and personal protective measures:

Mandatory lab coats, safety goggles, and nitrile gloves during chemical handling.
Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) briefings prior to chemical experimentation.
Emergency eyewash stations, fire blankets, and dry chemical extinguishers in every lab.
Segregated hazardous chemical waste disposal protocols in compliance with environmental standards.
